Lumio — Privacy Policy

This page explains what information the Lumio app keeps and why.

Camera

The camera and filters run on your phone. Photos and videos are not sent anywhere unless you tap “Send” yourself. Face tracking also happens on your phone; no face data is stored or sent. “Memories” stay only in your phone’s browser.

AI photo editing

When you use 🪄 AI edit, that one photo and your instruction are sent to an AI service (OpenAI or xAI Grok) to create the edited image. Lumio does not keep a copy.

Google login

When you log in, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture. Your name, username and picture are visible to other users in search. Your email address is never shown to anyone.

Friends, chat and stories

Your messages and the snaps you send are stored on Google Firebase and can only be seen by you and the friends you sent them to. A snap is deleted after it has been viewed. Stories can be seen only by your friends, for 24 hours.

Block and report

From someone’s profile you can block or report them. A report stores the problem you describe and that user’s ID so it can be reviewed.

Deleting your account

Profile → “Delete account” removes your profile, username, friendships and stories.

Children

This app is not intended for children under 13.
